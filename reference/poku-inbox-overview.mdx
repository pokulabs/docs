## Connect Your Twilio Account

To get started, plug in your Twilio credentials in the [Poku Inbox](https://inbox.pokulabs.com/). Once connected:

- All incoming and outgoing SMS messages will appear in your inbox
- Select any phone number to view its conversation history
- Send replies directly from the chat interface

---

## Taking Over a Conversation

With either the Make.com or n8n setup, you stay in control. When the AI agent toggle is **off**, Poku stops forwarding messages to the agent — so you can step in and handle the conversation directly from the Poku Inbox interface. Toggle the agent back on whenever you're ready to hand it back.
